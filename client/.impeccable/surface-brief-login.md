# Auth / login

<!-- impeccable:surface-brief 1 -->

## Scope
Sign-in and sign-up gate at `/login`. Landing deferred. Dashboard is a confirmation stub only.

## Mode
Operate

## Audience & job
A returning or new user needs to authenticate with Google and reach the dashboard without friction or decorative noise.

## Task
Toggle Sign in / Sign up copy, press Continue with Google, land on `/dashboard` on success; stay on `/login` with a clear error on failure.

## Direction
Inkan Seal Desk — fixed 420px column, washi/sumi/vermilion, seal press as the signature moment.

## Memorable moment
The vermilion Google CTA with amber step marker and seal-press motion.

## Unresolved
Email/password auth not in backend yet; Google-only for this gate.
